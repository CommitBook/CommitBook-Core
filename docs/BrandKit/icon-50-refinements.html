No fold feels calmer and avoids a second visual accent competing with the checked dot. The medium-light line lets the three nodes carry the story. This is a design preference, not a replacement of your chosen direction.

How light is too light?

The line mixes the chosen color toward white by 57%, 70%, or 82%. The third paper stays at 57%. I prefer 70% as the balance; 57% gives stronger continuity, while 82% can disappear at small sizes on a light canvas.

About the same-color fold

Without a color change, a fold reads primarily as a clipped corner. The bottom row adds a fine crease without recoloring the folded area. All options retain live recoloring and square app-icon source exports.
